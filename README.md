# Stone Age — 2D Cartoon Video Starter

A cheerful, kid-friendly animated short introducing everyday life in the Stone Age. The storyboard and timed narration are in [`storyboard.md`](storyboard.md).

## Video concept

- **Working title:** *A Day in the Stone Age*
- **Length:** About 90 seconds
- **Audience:** Children and families
- **Look:** Colorful 2D storybook animation, warm earthy palette, expressive characters, simple shapes, gentle motion
- **Tone:** Curious, friendly, and respectful—people in the past were inventive and skilled

## Story in one sentence

A curious young hunter-gatherer named Nia explores her community's day, learning how people made tools, gathered food, used fire, and shared stories through art.

## Production notes

- Keep narration short and easy to understand; let the pictures tell the story.
- Show a small community working together rather than relying on the “caveman” stereotype.
- Stone Age life varied across places and thousands of years. Treat the characters and setting as a fictional introduction, not a depiction of one specific culture or date.
- Use gentle sound effects and music; avoid scary hunting scenes.
- Suggested format: 16:9, 1080p, 24 fps. Add captions for narration.

## Suggested workflow

1. Record the narration from the storyboard.
2. Create character and background designs that stay consistent from shot to shot.
3. Make a rough animatic using the storyboard timings.
4. Animate, add sound and captions, then export an MP4.

See `storyboard.md` for the timed script, shot list, and visual direction.
